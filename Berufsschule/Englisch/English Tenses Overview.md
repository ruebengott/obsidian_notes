# English Tenses Overview

> [!summary] Kurz & knapp
> Englische Zeitformen ergeben sich aus **Zeit** (Past / Present / Future) × **Aspekt** (Simple / Continuous / Perfect / Perfect Continuous). Bildung: Continuous = *be + -ing*, Perfect = *have + Partizip (3. Form)*, Perfect Continuous = *have + been + -ing*.

## Übersicht (Beispielverb: *cook*)
| | Past | Present | Future |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Simple** | *cooked* – Handlung zu einem Zeitpunkt in der Vergangenheit abgeschlossen. *He cooked yesterday.* | *cook / cooks* – Zustand, Gewohnheit, Wiederholung. *He cooks dinner every Friday.* | *will cook* – Plan/Vorhersage. *He will cook tomorrow.* |
| **Continuous** (be + -ing) | *was / were cooking* – lief gerade, als etwas anderes passierte. *He was cooking when the phone rang.* | *am / is / are cooking* – passiert gerade jetzt (oder fester Plan). *He is cooking now.* | *will be cooking* – wird in der Zukunft gerade ablaufen. *He will be cooking when you come.* |
| **Perfect** (have + 3. Form) | *had cooked* – vor einer anderen vergangenen Handlung abgeschlossen. *He had cooked the dinner when the phone rang.* | *has / have cooked* – zu unbestimmtem Zeitpunkt in der Vergangenheit, Bezug zur Gegenwart. *He has cooked many meals.* | *will have cooked* – wird vor einem Zeitpunkt in der Zukunft abgeschlossen sein. *He will have cooked dinner by the time you come.* |
| **Perfect Continuous** (have + been + -ing) | *had been cooking* – dauerte bis zu einem Punkt in der Vergangenheit an. *He had been cooking for a long time before he took lessons.* | *has / have been cooking* – begann in der Vergangenheit, dauert bis jetzt an. *He has been cooking for over an hour.* | *will have been cooking* – wird bis zu einem Zukunftspunkt angedauert haben. *He will have been cooking all day by the time she gets home.* |

## Signalwörter (time clues)
| Zeitform | typische Signalwörter |
|---|---|
| Simple Present | always, usually, often, every day, once a week |
| Present Continuous | now, right now, at the moment, currently |
| Simple Past | yesterday, last week, ago, in 2020 |
| Past Continuous | while, when (+ Simple Past) |
| Present Perfect | already, yet, ever, never, just, recently, so far |
| Present Perfect Continuous | for, since, all day, how long |
| Past Perfect | before, after, by the time (Vergangenheit) |
| Future (will / going to) | tomorrow, next week, soon, tonight |
| Future Perfect | by the time, by 5 pm, by next year |

## Konjugation von *to be* (Arbeitsblatt „to be“)
| Zeitform | I | you / we / they | he / she / it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simple Present | am | are | is |
| Simple Past | was | were | was |
| Present Progressive | am being | are being | is being |
| Past Progressive | was being | were being | was being |
| Future *will* | will be | will be | will be |
| Future *going to* | am going to be | are going to be | is going to be |
| Present Perfect | have been | have been | has been |
| Past Perfect | had been | had been | had been |
| Future Perfect *will* | will have been | will have been | will have been |
| Future Perfect *going to* | am going to have been | are going to have been | is going to have been |

Bei den übrigen Progressive-Formen ändert sich nur das Vollverb, *to be* bleibt gleich:
- Future Progressive: *I will be doing / He will be doing.*
- Present Perfect Progressive: *I have been doing / He has been doing.*
- Past Perfect Progressive: *I had been doing / He had been doing.*
- Future Perfect Progressive: *I will have been doing / He will have been doing.*

> [!note] Hinweis
> Auf dem Arbeitsblatt steht „Present **Prefect**“ – gemeint ist **Present Perfect**. „Simple Present Progressive“ heißt üblicherweise einfach **Present Progressive / Present Continuous** (progressive = continuous).

> [!tip] Merke
> Die Formen von **to be** brauchst du für **Continuous** und **Passive** – wer *to be* in allen Zeiten kann, kann auch das [[Passive Voice|Passiv]] bilden.

## Lernfragen
> [!question]- Wie bildet man das Present Perfect Continuous?
> *have/has + been + Verb-ing* – z. B. *He has been cooking for over an hour.*

> [!question]- Wann benutzt man das Past Perfect?
> Für eine Handlung, die **vor** einer anderen vergangenen Handlung abgeschlossen war: *He had cooked the dinner when the phone rang.*

> [!question]- Welche Zeitform: „He will have cooked dinner by the time you come.“?
> Future Perfect (*will have + 3. Form*).

> [!question]- Welche Form von *to be*: „they“ im Simple Past?
> *they were*
