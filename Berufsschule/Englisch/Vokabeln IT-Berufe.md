# Vokabeln IT-Berufe

> [!summary] Kurz & knapp
> Word Bank vom Arbeitsblatt „People and jobs in IT“ (IT024) plus wichtige Begriffe aus den Job-Beschreibungen.

## Word Bank (vom Arbeitsblatt)
| English | Deutsch | Beispiel |
|---|---|---|
| database | Datenbank | *I integrate SQL Server databases into websites.* |
| IT consultant | IT-Berater/in | *An IT consultant advises clients on costs and risks.* |
| LAN (local area network) | lokales Netzwerk | *He maintains our LAN.* |
| network | (Computer-)Netzwerk | *A network connects computers.* |
| network administrator | Netzwerkadministrator/in | *The network administrator troubleshoots the network.* |
| programmer | Programmierer/in | *A programmer develops new applications.* |
| systems analyst | Systemberater/in, Systemanalytiker/in | *A systems analyst analyses business requirements.* |
| WAN (wide area network) | Weitverkehrsnetz | *The WAN connects our branch offices.* |
| web developer | Webentwickler/in | *A web developer designs websites.* |
| website | Internetseite, Website | *We redesigned our company website.* |

## Aus den Texten
| English | Deutsch | Beispiel |
|---|---|---|
| responsibility | Zuständigkeit, Verantwortung | *Her responsibilities include server maintenance.* |
| skill | Fähigkeit, Fertigkeit | *strong communication skills* |
| qualification | Qualifikation, Abschluss | *He has the necessary qualifications.* |
| requirement | Anforderung, Voraussetzung | *What are the requirements for this job?* |
| to troubleshoot | Fehler suchen und beheben | *I troubleshoot networks.* |
| to upgrade | aufrüsten, aktualisieren | *We upgraded the switches.* |
| maintenance | Wartung | *maintenance of our LAN/WAN* |
| to keep up to date with | auf dem Laufenden bleiben | *I keep up to date with new trends.* |
| to advise sb. on sth. | jdn. zu etw. beraten | *I advise my clients on costs.* |
| benefit | Nutzen, Vorteil | *costs, benefits and risks* |
| confidential | vertraulich | *The information is confidential.* |
| certificate | Zertifikat | *internationally recognized certificates* |
| implementation | Umsetzung, Implementierung | *from the first idea to implementation* |
| verbal / written | mündlich / schriftlich | *verbal and written communication skills* |

> [!tip] Merke
> *to advise* (Verb, mit **s** gesprochen [z]) ↔ *advice* (Nomen, mit **c**, unzählbar: *a piece of advice*).

## Lernfragen
> [!question]- What's the English word for „Fehler suchen und beheben“?
> *to troubleshoot*

> [!question]- What's the English word for „vertraulich“?
> *confidential*

> [!question]- What's the English word for „Weitverkehrsnetz“?
> *WAN – wide area network*

> [!question]- What's the English word for „Wartung“?
> *maintenance*
