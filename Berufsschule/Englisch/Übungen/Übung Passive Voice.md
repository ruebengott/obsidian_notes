# Übung Passive Voice

> [!summary] Kurz & knapp
> *Task: Identify and highlight the verb, including its tense, in the given sentence. State the correct tense and then rewrite the sentence in the passive voice.* – 16 Sätze mit geprüfter Lösung.

![[Technisches Englisch.pdf#page=12]]

## Lösungen (geprüft und korrigiert)
Die Mitschrift (S. 13–14) enthielt eine Musterlösung; drei Passivsätze darin waren fehlerhaft (siehe ⚠).

| Nr. | Satz (Aktiv) | Verb – Tense | Passiv |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | George had repaired many cars before he received his mechanic's license. | *had repaired* – past perfect | Many cars **had been repaired** (by George) before he received his mechanic's license. |
| 2 | Sally is going to make a beautiful dinner tonight. | *is going to make* – future (going to) | A beautiful dinner **is going to be made** tonight. |
| 3 | Recently, John has been doing the work. | *has been doing* – present perfect continuous | Ersatzform: The work **has been done** recently. |
| 4 | Once a week, Tom cleans the house. | *cleans* – simple present | The house **is cleaned** once a week. |
| 5 | Someone will finish the work by 5:00 PM. | *will finish* – future (will) | The work **will be finished** by 5:00 PM. |
| 6 | At 8:00 PM tonight, John will be washing the dishes. | *will be washing* – future continuous | Ersatzform: The dishes **will be washed** at 8:00 PM tonight. |
| 7 | Many tourists have visited that castle. | *have visited* – present perfect | That castle **has been visited** by many tourists. |
| 8 | The famous artist will have been painting the mural for over six months by the time it is finished. | *will have been painting* – future perfect continuous | kein natürliches Passiv |
| 9 ⚠ | The salesman was helping the customer when the thief came into the store. | *was helping* – past continuous | The customer **was being helped** when the thief came into the store. |
| 10 ⚠ | At 8:00 PM tonight, John is going to be washing the dishes. | *is going to be washing* – future continuous (going to) | Ersatzform: The dishes **are going to be washed** at 8:00 PM tonight. |
| 11 ⚠ | Chef Jones had been preparing the restaurant's fantastic dinners for two years. | *had been preparing* – past perfect continuous | Ersatzform: The restaurant's fantastic dinners **had been prepared** by Chef Jones for two years. |
| 12 | Right now, Sarah is writing the letter. | *is writing* – present continuous | The letter **is being written** right now. |
| 13 | Sam repaired the car. | *repaired* – simple past | The car **was repaired** (by Sam). |
| 14 | They will have completed the project by the time you arrive. | *will have completed* – future perfect | The project **will have been completed** by the time you arrive. |
| 15 | They are going to have completed the project by the time you arrive. | *are going to have completed* – future perfect (going to) | The project **is going to have been completed** by the time you arrive. |
| 16 | The famous artist is going to have been painting the mural for over six months by the time it is finished. | *is going to have been painting* – future perfect continuous (going to) | kein natürliches Passiv |

## Korrekturen zur Mitschrift
| Nr. | In der Mitschrift | Korrektur | Warum |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (handschriftlich) | *Many cars were …* | *Many cars **had been** repaired* | Zeitform bleibt Past Perfect |
| 9 | *was **been** being helped* | *was being helped* | *was been* gibt es nicht; Past Continuous Passiv = *was/were being* + 3. Form |
| 10 | *are going to be being washed* | *are going to be washed* | Doppel-*be* ist ungebräuchlich |
| 11 | *had been being prepared* | *had been prepared* | Perfect Continuous Passiv wird praktisch nicht verwendet |

Außerdem stammten Quellen-Reste wie „[englishwithashish +1]“, „[Anhang +1]“ aus einem KI-Chat – entfernt.

> [!tip] Merke
> Aktiv-Zeitform bestimmen → dieselbe Zeitform von **to be** bilden → **3. Form** anhängen.

## Lernfragen
> [!question]- Passiv von „The salesman was helping the customer“?
> *The customer was being helped (by the salesman).*

> [!question]- Passiv von „They will have completed the project“?
> *The project will have been completed.*

> [!question]- Was macht man mit Perfect-Continuous-Sätzen im Passiv?
> Kein natürliches Passiv – man weicht auf die Perfect-Form aus (*has been done*, *had been prepared*).
