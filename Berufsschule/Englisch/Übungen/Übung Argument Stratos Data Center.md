# Übung Argument Stratos Data Center

> [!summary] Kurz & knapp
> Anwendung des Argument-Schemas ([[Parts of an Argument]]) auf das geplante KI-Rechenzentrum „Stratos“ in Utah. Claim: Die Abwärme schadet Klima und Ökosystem im Tal. Die **Concession fehlte** noch – unten ein Vorschlag.

![[Technisches Englisch.pdf#page=22]]

## Dein Text – korrigiert
> **Introduction:** The **project** „Stratos“ in Utah **is planned to be** the world's biggest AI data **center**, **covering** more than **16,000** hectares **with** 9 **gigawatts** of power.
>
> **Claim:** The proposed Stratos data center in Box Elder County will seriously harm the local climate and ecosystem by pumping huge amounts of waste heat into a narrow valley.
>
> **Evidence:** Plans call for about 9 GW of power and roughly 7–8 GW of waste heat, which researchers say equals the daily heat of **around** 23 atomic bombs.
>
> **Warrant:** When that much continuous heat is released into a bowl-shaped valley, it can trap warm air and disturb normal night cooling and moisture cycles.
>
> **Backing:** Scientists warn that this could damage soil crusts, harm species, **encourage** invasive plants and **increase** the fire risk.
>
> **Rebuttal:** Developers say **that technology** and cleaner power can fix this, but the scale and local geography **mean that these fixes are not enough** to fully prevent harm without much stricter safeguards and transparent plans.
>
> **Concession (Vorschlag):** *Admittedly, the data center would create jobs and tax revenue for the region, and modern cooling systems can reduce part of the waste heat. However, these benefits do not outweigh the long-term damage to the valley's climate and ecosystem.*

## Typische Fehler
| Original | Korrektur | Warum |
|---|---|---|
| roundabout 23 | **around / roughly / about** 23 | *roundabout* = Kreisverkehr bzw. „umständlich“ |
| enhance the fire risk | **increase** the fire risk | *enhance* = verbessern (positiv) |
| make those fixes not appropriate to … | **mean that these fixes are not enough to …** | idiomatischer, klarere Struktur |
| 16000, 9 Gigawatt | **16,000**, 9 **gigawatts** | Tausendertrennzeichen Komma; Einheiten klein, Plural |
| data Center | data **center** | kein Großbuchstabe bei Nomen |
| world‘s | world**'**s | englischer Apostroph |
| tech | **technology** | formeller Text → kein Slang |

> [!warning] Inhalt
> Zahlen und Aussagen (Fläche, GW, „23 Atombomben“) stammen aus deiner Recherche und wurden hier nicht geprüft.

> [!tip] Merke
> Jeder Argumentationstext braucht am Ende eine **Concession**: Gegenseite fair anerkennen (*Admittedly …*), dann mit *However …* zurück zur eigenen These.

## Lernfragen
> [!question]- Welcher Teil fehlte in deinem Argument?
> Die Concession & Qualification.

> [!question]- Was ist falsch an „roundabout 23 atomic bombs“?
> *roundabout* bedeutet Kreisverkehr – richtig: *around / roughly / about*.

> [!question]- Welche Rolle hat der Satz „When that much continuous heat is released into a bowl-shaped valley, it can trap warm air …“?
> Warrant – er erklärt, warum die Abwärme (evidence) zum Schaden (claim) führt.
